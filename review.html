Final review

The final review is the last step to your certificate. It contains 20 questions from all seven modules. The questions are mixed, so you must recognize the subject of each question yourself. This is a better test of what you know than one quiz for each module.

1

How the final review works

  • Each try contains approximately 3 questions from each module. The questions and the order of the answers change each time.
  • You must get 75% or more (15 of 20) to pass. This is more than the 70% of a module knowledge check.
  • After each answer, an explanation tells you which module and section to read again.
  • You can try again as many times as you want. Your best score is kept.
Note

Do the knowledge check of each module first. To get the certificate, you must pass all seven knowledge checks and the final review. Your knowledge checks:

    Before you start, read the cheat sheets. They contain the most important points of each module on one page.
